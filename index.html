A high-retention study loop

Study method—not slide marathons

Your preferred workflow, shaped by retrieval practice and spacing.
Evidence-informed

One repeatable loop

  1. Retrieve first: 2–3 minutes of “what do I already know?” before opening the video.
  2. Watch actively: pause to predict a mechanism or redraw a pathway; avoid verbatim notes.
  3. Close the source: explain the topic from memory in a few bullets or a quick diagram.
  4. Immediately do: the linked Anki cards and quiz questions; log misses in one short error list.
  5. Space it: revisit misses tomorrow, then later in the week; keep old cards in scattered 50-card blocks.
  6. Final 2–3 days: timed UWorld, deliberate explanation review, and targeted First Aid lookup—not a cover-to-cover slide reread.
